Bike Sales Analysis: Understanding Customer Purchase Behaviour

Scenario

A regional bike retailer wanted to improve its sales strategy by identifying key customer demographics most likely to purchase bikes. They collected customer data over the past year, including personal details, income levels, commuting habits, and purchasing behavior.

Download & view project file here
The dataset used for this project can be found here

Data Attributes Analysed

  • Demographics: Age, Gender, Marital Status, Number of Children
  • Financial & Work Details: Income, Occupation, Education Level
  • Lifestyle Factors: Homeownership, Number of Cars, Commute Distance
  • Location: Region
  • Purchase Behavior: Whether a bike was purchased

Analysis Goals

  • ✅ Which customer profiles are most likely to purchase a bike?
  • ✅ How do factors like income, marital status, or commute distance impact bike sales?
  • ✅ What trends exist across different age groups and regions?
  • ✅ How can the retailer tailor its marketing and inventory based on these insights?

Methods Used

  • Tool: Microsoft Excel",
  • Data cleaning & preparation — removed duplicates and formatted data for analysis. Used IFS function to simplify age data",
  • Pivot tables & charts — Created pivot tables to summarise sales trends by demographics and generated visual insights",
  • Dashboards — designed an interactive dashboard (includes slicers) to visualise findings dynamically"

Key Findings

  • 📊 High Earning Middle-aged men with a short commute distance are more likely to purchase a bike.
  • 📊 Married customers with children were less likely to purchase, preferring cars for commuting.
  • 📊 In Europe, young adults with a shorter commute distance are more likely to purchase bikes. In North America, less people purchase bikes overall, but, of those who do, middle-aged men are the main demographic. In the Pacific, more people purchase bikes than don't, with customers being in the middle-age or young adult demographic

Conclusion

  • The retailer could improve sales by targeting ads toward commuters in high-income regions.
  • Create more children's bikes to target Married customers with children.
  • Further investigate why there is a huge drop of buyers that commute 1-2 miles and then another spike at 5-10 miles.
